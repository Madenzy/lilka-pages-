1Who these terms apply to

These terms apply to you if you list events or offer appointments on Lilka (“you”). They are in addition to our terms of use, privacy policy and community guidelines. If anything conflicts, these terms win for your organiser or business account.

2Eligibility and checks

  • You must be 18 or over, and able to enter into contracts for yourself or your business.
  • You must verify your email address and phone number before you can create events.
  • Your public profile (name, contact email, website or social links) must be accurate. Buyers see it on your event pages.
  • To sell paid tickets, you must complete Stripe’s onboarding. Stripe checks your identity and bank details on our behalf. We don’t store your ID documents or bank details.
  • Your account shows a verification status: unverified, pending, verified or suspended. We may ask for more information at any time.
  • You must accept the latest version of these terms. We record which version you accepted and when.

3Your listings

  • Give an accurate title, date, time, venue, price and description. Use photos you have the right to use.
  • Don’t sell more tickets than the venue can safely hold.
  • Show any age limit and choose a refund policy. Both are shown to buyers before they pay.
  • Describe access arrangements, such as step-free entry, so disabled attendees can plan.
  • Your first events are reviewed by our team before they go live, usually within [1 working day]. Once you have a good track record, we may let your events publish straight away.
  • We may decline or remove listings that break these terms or our community guidelines, and we’ll tell you why.

4Licences, safety and insurance

You are responsible for running your event safely and legally. When you list an event, you confirm the following. We may ask for proof.

  • Alcohol and entertainment licences. If you sell alcohol or put on regulated entertainment (such as live or recorded music) at a venue without its own premises licence, you may need a Temporary Event Notice from the local council. A Temporary Event Notice covers fewer than 500 people at any one time, including staff, and must be given to the council in advance.
  • Music licensing. Playing recorded or live music in public usually needs a licence from PPL PRS (TheMusicLicence), unless the venue already has one.
  • Health and safety. Carry out a risk assessment, including fire safety and crowd management, that suits the size of your event.
  • Food. Anyone selling food must be registered with their local council as a food business.
  • Insurance. Public liability insurance is required for events with a capacity of 500 or more, and strongly recommended for all events.
Check the rules

Licensing rules are set by local councils and change from time to time. Check the current requirements on gov.uk and with your council before you sell tickets.

6Refunds, cancellations and chargebacks

  • You must honour the refund policy you chose for the event.
  • If you cancel an event, every buyer is refunded the full ticket price automatically. [Say whether Lilka’s fee is also refunded and who pays it.]
  • If you move an event or change it significantly, buyers must be offered a refund.
  • If a buyer disputes a payment with their bank (a chargeback) because of a problem with your event, the amount and any bank fee may be taken from your payouts.

7Appointment bookings

  • Keep your services, prices, opening hours and staff availability up to date, so customers can only book times you can honour.
  • Honour confirmed bookings. If you have to cancel or move one, contact the customer as soon as possible.
  • Only charge a late-cancellation or no-show fee if it was shown to the customer before they booked.

8Customer and attendee data

We pass you the details customers and attendees give when they book or buy (such as name, email, phone number and booking notes). For that data you are a separate controller under UK data protection law. You must:

  • Use it only to run your event or appointment and to contact people about it.
  • Not send marketing unless the person has separately agreed to hear from you.
  • Keep it secure, share it only with staff who need it, and delete it when you no longer need it.
  • Respond to people who ask to see or delete their data.
  • Tell us at privacy@lilka.app within 24 hours if you have a data breach involving data from Lilka.
  • Keep staff and check-in logins private. Remove staff from your account when they leave.

9Reports, reviews and enforcement

Anyone can report an event. When several reports come in, ticket sales are paused automatically until our team has reviewed the event. We may then ask you to make changes, remove the listing, refund buyers, hold payouts or suspend your account.

Every action we take is recorded with who took it and why. If you disagree with a decision, email safety@lilka.app within 14 days and someone who wasn’t involved will look at it again.

10Ending your account

You can close your organiser or business account once your events have finished and any refunds and payouts are settled. We may close it under section 9 of these terms or our terms of use. We keep financial records for as long as the law requires, as set out in our privacy policy.
