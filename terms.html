1About these terms

2What Lilka does

Lilka helps you find events, buy tickets and book appointments with local businesses. We provide the website, take payments and send your confirmations and tickets.

The event or appointment is provided by the organiser or business, not by Lilka. Your contract for the event or service is with them, and they are responsible for delivering it as described. When you pay for a ticket, we collect the money on the organiser’s behalf.

3Your account

  • You must be 18 or over.
  • Give accurate details and keep them up to date.
  • Keep your password private. You’re responsible for what happens on your account until you tell us it has been misused.
  • If you think someone else has used your account, change your password and email support@lilka.app straight away.
  • You can close your account at any time. Tickets you’ve already bought stay valid.

You can also book appointments and buy tickets as a guest, without an account.

4Booking appointments

  • Your booking is with the business. We email a confirmation with your booking reference (for example LK-7F3K9Q).
  • Unless the booking page says otherwise, you pay the business in person on the day.
  • You can cancel for free up to 24 hours before your appointment using Manage my booking.
  • If you cancel later than that or don’t turn up, the business may charge a fee, but only if they told you about it before you booked.
  • If the business needs to move or cancel your appointment, they will contact you as soon as they can.

5Buying tickets

  • The price you see before paying includes VAT and any booking fee ([booking fee, e.g. 5% + 49p per paid ticket, or none]).
  • Your order is confirmed when we email your tickets. Each ticket has its own QR code.
  • Each QR code lets one person in, once. Keep it private: if someone else uses it first, the organiser may not let you in.
  • Bring photo ID to events with an age limit.
  • Organisers can refuse entry, or ask people to leave, for safety reasons, age limits or behaviour. Whether you get a refund then depends on their event conditions and the reason.
  • We may limit how many tickets you can buy in one order.

6Cancellations, changes and refunds

Full details are in our refund policy. The key points:

  • Event cancelled: you get the ticket price back automatically.
  • Event moved or changed a lot (new date, new venue or main act gone): you can choose to keep your tickets or get a refund.
  • Otherwise: the organiser’s refund policy applies. It is shown on the event page before you pay.
Your cooling-off rights

The 14-day cancellation period for online purchases doesn’t apply to tickets for leisure events on a specific date. This is set out in the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. Your other consumer rights still apply.

7Reselling tickets

Tickets are for your own use or to give to people you know. Please don’t resell them for more than you paid, or through websites the organiser hasn’t approved.

We may cancel tickets bought with automated software (bots), or resold in breach of these terms or the organiser’s conditions. If you can’t go, contact the organiser first.
