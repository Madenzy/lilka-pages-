In short

  • We collect what we need to run your bookings and tickets, keep your account secure and meet the law. Nothing more.
  • Card details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • The business or organiser you book with gets your name and contact details so they can look after you.
  • We never sell your data and we don’t use advertising trackers.
  • You can see, correct, download or delete your data by emailing privacy@lilka.app.

2Who this policy covers

  • Customers who book appointments with businesses on Lilka, including guests who book without an account.
  • Ticket buyers and attendees, including people whose name is on a ticket someone else bought.
  • Organisers and business owners, plus any staff they add to Lilka.
  • Visitors browsing the site without signing in.

5Who we share it with

  • The business or organiser you book with. They need your name and contact details to provide the appointment or event. They become responsible for that data too, and their own privacy notice applies to what they do with it.
  • Stripe, which processes card payments and refunds and runs identity checks on organisers who get paid. Stripe has its own privacy policy.
  • Companies that help us run Lilka, such as our hosting provider ([hosting provider]), email provider ([email provider]) and text message provider ([SMS provider]). They can only use the data to provide their service to us, under a written contract.
  • Google Fonts. Our font is loaded from Google’s servers, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads. We plan to host the font ourselves so this stops.
  • The police, courts or regulators, but only when the law requires it or to prevent fraud or harm.
  • A buyer of our business, if Lilka is ever sold. They would have to keep to this policy.

We never sell personal data.

6Data sent outside the UK

Some of the companies above, including Stripe and Google, may handle data outside the UK. When that happens it is protected either because the UK government has approved that country’s data protection laws, or by the UK’s International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, which contractually requires the same level of protection.

8How we protect it

  • Everything is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are scrambled with a one-way hash, so even we can’t read them.
  • Card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers.
  • Staff and organisers only see what their role needs. Important changes are recorded with who made them.
  • We watch for attacks such as repeated failed sign-ins and lock accounts when we see them.

If a data breach puts your rights at risk, we will report it to the ICO within 72 hours where the law requires, and tell you without undue delay if the risk to you is high.

9Your rights

You have the right to:

  • See your data and get a copy of it.
  • Correct it if it’s wrong or incomplete.
  • Have it deleted, unless we must keep it by law (such as tax records).
  • Limit how we use it while a complaint or correction is sorted out.
  • Object to us using it based on legitimate interests. You can always object to marketing, and we will stop.
  • Take it with you in a common format such as a spreadsheet.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, for anything we do with your consent.
  • Not be subject to decisions made only by a computer that have a significant effect on you. We don’t make decisions like that. When an event is paused automatically after reports, a person always reviews it.

To use any of these rights, email privacy@lilka.app. It’s free. We may ask you to confirm who you are first. We’ll reply within one month, or tell you within that month if a complex request needs up to two more months.

10Children

You must be 18 or over to create an account, book an appointment or buy tickets. Younger people can attend events when an adult buys their ticket. If an event has an age limit, the organiser checks ID at the door.

If you think a child has given us their details, email privacy@lilka.app and we’ll delete them.

11Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, please tell us first at privacy@lilka.app. We’ll acknowledge your complaint, look into it and let you know the outcome.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at any time: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

12Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data in a significant way, we’ll email you before it takes effect.
