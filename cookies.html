  • We only use cookies that are strictly necessary, so we don’t need to ask before setting them.

  • If that ever changes, we’ll ask for your permission first and update this page.

1What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. Browser storage (often called local storage) works in a similar way. Both let a site remember things between pages, such as whether you’re signed in.

UK law lets websites use cookies without asking if they are strictly necessary for a service you’ve asked for. Every cookie on Lilka falls into that group.

3What we don’t use

  • Advertising or retargeting cookies
  • Social media pixels (such as Meta or TikTok)
  • Analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics)
  • Anything that follows you across other websites

If we add analytics in future, we’ll show a cookie banner that asks for your permission first, with ‘Reject’ as easy to choose as ‘Accept’, and list the new cookies here.

4Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the strictly necessary ones, you won’t be able to sign in or check out.

To clear Lilka’s browser storage, clear site data for lilka.app in your browser settings, or use the Reset demo link in the dashboards.
